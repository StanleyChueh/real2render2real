## Observations relevant to later research (not bugs, not fixed)

These were found while validating the data and are recorded so they can be studied later. None were changed.

- **O-1 IK joint jumps at trajectory start.** `yumi_joint_6_r` moves ≈1 rad within a few control steps around t≈10 (median over 60 episodes), and the per-episode max single-step joint change has p99 ≈ 0.67 rad. This is consistent with an IK branch switch (jaxmp differential IK) at the transition from the setup phase to the tracked trajectory.
- **O-2 Single-arm usage.** In the coffee-maker task, all sampled episodes use the right arm; the left arm stays at a jittered home pose. Bimanual behaviour is not exercised by this task.
- **O-3 Baked scan artifacts.** A dark, semi-transparent blob moves with the mug in renders (visible in both cameras). It looks like table/background floaters from 3DGS segmentation baked into the SuGaR mesh texture, not a shadow (shadows are disabled in `RenderCfg`).
- **O-4 "Success" = kinematic feasibility.** Rollouts are rejected only if (a) EE z < 2.5 cm, (b) IK tracking error > 2 cm or > 0.45 rad, or (c) joint limits are exceeded. There is no physics/contact check of task success (by design: "without dynamics simulation").
- **O-5 Fixed episode length.** Every rollout has exactly T = 161 logged steps (setup phase excluded), independent of the demonstration speed.
